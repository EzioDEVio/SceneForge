## SceneForge Studio 0.7.0

The biggest update since 0.5.3: a multitrack audio timeline with pro edit tools, 71 caption styles, 400 stickers and emoji, per-effect settings, and optional AI tools that run on your own PC.

### Download
| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | `SceneForge-Studio-0.7.0-Windows-x64-Setup.exe` |
| Linux | `.AppImage` or `.deb` |
| macOS (Apple Silicon, experimental) | `.dmg` |

On Windows, *"Windows protected your PC"* may appear. Click **More info → Run anyway**; the app has no paid code-signing certificate. 0.5.3 users are offered this update automatically.

### What's new
- **Timeline:**
  - Audio tracks A3–A8 with mute, solo and lock.
  - Tools: Select, Ripple, Roll, Slip, Slide, Blade and Razor All.
  - Right-click cut, copy, paste and delete. Delete removes only the piece you cut, never the video.
  - Detach narration or clip sound, then cut it like any clip.
  - Clip groups.
  - Colour, range, clip-attached and beat markers.
  - Volume envelopes.
- **Captions and text:**
  - 71 caption styles, including Arabic styles.
  - Free local Whisper captions, now working on Windows.
  - Clock, iris and directional text reveals.
  - Textured titles: lava, neon, gold and more, or your own image or prompt.
- **Effects:**
  - Settings for 36 looks.
  - Film grades and halation, VHS, lens flare, camera shake, wiggle, focus blur, tilt-shift and mosaic.
  - Effect stack ordering.
  - Look preset packs, with a starter pack included.
- **Stickers:** 400 stickers and emoji, searchable, in 14 categories.
- **AI on your PC (optional, downloads once):**
  - Subject cutout for photos and moving video ("text behind subject").
  - Background removal.
  - Voice isolation.
- **Audio:** beat-aware music fit, dialogue cleanup, and scene renders that include your timeline audio and music.
- **AI generation (optional):**
  - Text-to-video with local ComfyUI or Google Veo / Runway, with a cost estimate and confirmation before anything is charged.
  - Image Studio.
- **Local voices:** start Chatterbox or Kokoro from inside the app (needs Docker Desktop).
