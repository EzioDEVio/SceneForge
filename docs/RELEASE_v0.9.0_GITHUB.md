## SceneForge Studio 0.9.0: Friendlier editing

Fixes from the 0.8.0 test, a tidier Effects tab and new time-savers. Everything from 0.8.0 is included.

### Download
| System | File |
|---|---|
| Windows 10/11 (64-bit) | `SceneForge-Studio-0.9.0-Windows-x64-Setup.exe` |
| Linux | `.AppImage` or `.deb` |
| macOS (Apple Silicon, experimental) | `.dmg` |

On Windows, *"Windows protected your PC"* may appear. Click **More info → Run anyway**. Existing users are offered this update automatically.

### What's new
- **Typewriter in its own box:** switch, speed, timing, keystroke sound, volume and a **Preview sound** button, all in one place in the Text tab.
- **Effects tab sorted into groups** (Colour grading, Light & lens, Camera & motion, Point things out, Layout & privacy, Scene intro, Combine & reuse looks). Groups with an effect switched on open by themselves.
- **Waveforms** on A2 clip sound and on A3–A8 timeline clips.
- **Auto-ducking for A3–A8:** music and sound effects lower themselves under narration and clip sound.
- **Restore points:** automatic every few minutes, plus named ones. Restoring opens a copy, so nothing is overwritten.
- **Try a sample project** on the Projects page.
- **Caption translation to English**, or bilingual captions (spoken + English), with free Local Whisper.
- **Title templates:** Lower third, Intro, Outro, Chapter heading, Quote.

### Fixes
- The ♪ Beats menu text is no longer cut off on Windows.
- Track names no longer overlap the M / S buttons, and the editor's left edge is no longer cut off.

The full notes are in [desktop/RELEASE_NOTES.md](https://github.com/EzioDEVio/SceneForge/blob/main/desktop/RELEASE_NOTES.md).
