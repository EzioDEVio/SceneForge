# Code of conduct

SceneForge is made by people with different languages, backgrounds and levels of experience. We want everyone who uses or contributes to it to feel welcome.

**Expected:** be kind and patient, assume good intent, give feedback about the work and not the person, and help newcomers find their way.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults or personal attacks, discriminatory language or imagery, sexual content, publishing someone's private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

**Where this applies:** issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

**Reporting:** contact the maintainer privately (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for how to reach them without posting publicly). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block people who break this code.

This code is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
